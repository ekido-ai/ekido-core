# Filtered invoice and FAQ distillation, 20 November 2026

These follow-up runs use the invoice-target and FAQ answer-echo filters in
[workers #18](https://github.com/ekido-ai/ekido-workers/pull/18), after the
data audit recorded in [core #88](https://github.com/ekido-ai/ekido-core/pull/88).
They contribute to [issue #33](https://github.com/ekido-ai/ekido-internal/issues/33).
Each started from 30 labelled seeds and trained the 1.5B student for 50 steps
on accepted examples generated by the 4-bit 7B teacher.

| Recipe | Generated rows | Base pass rate | Student pass rate | Recipe floor |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Invoice | 36 | 0/50 | 50/50 | 0.70 |
| Clinic FAQ | 88 | 0/60 | 7/60 | 0.60 |

The invoice trainer rejected 48 generated rows for missing or leaked target
values. The FAQ trainer rejected 28 answer echoes and 24 prompt-marker rows.
The accepted invoice rows passed the literal field-presence check; the
accepted FAQ rows contained neither a verbatim full answer nor a `New
Question:` marker. These checks do not establish semantic correctness.

The saved step-50 checkpoints were loaded in a separate T4 execution, which
reproduced both trainer scores. The 110 individual predictions are in
`reevaluation/`; each pass/fail result was independently recomputed with the
project scorer. Full checkpoints, including optimizer tensors, were downloaded
locally and matched their manifests; the large weights are also retained in
the private source notebooks rather than committed to Git. The dataset hashes,
checkpoint lineage, deterministic 30-seed selection, exact train/seed/test
input separation and committed test-set identities were verified.

These are reused synthetic tests, not a fresh independent test set. In
particular, [core #74](https://github.com/ekido-ai/ekido-core/issues/74)
identifies the invoice test as highly templated and unsuitable for claims
about real customer invoices. A further literal similarity check found no
exact generated/train-to-test input or target duplicates; the highest sequence
similarity between a generated invoice input and a test input was 0.746. The
perfect invoice score should be understood in that context. FAQ remains below
its quality floor.

## FAQ factual mismatch audit

The FAQ scorer uses token overlap and a 0.4 per-example ROUGE-L pass mark;
it does not check whether the answer's facts agree with the reference. In
the seven reported FAQ passes, manual inspection identified at least three
clear contradictions: a 6 pm rather than 5 pm closing time, an answer
saying the clinic is open on Sundays when the reference says closed, and a
fee of 100 TL instead of 400 rupees. Their individual scores still exceed 0.4.
The examples are fictional, but this demonstrates that a pass can include an
incorrect fact; the 7/60 result must not be treated as seven factually correct
answers. `faq-factual-audit.json` records the cases and
`python experiments/results/filtered-distillation-20261120/faq-factual-audit.py`
reproduces the scorer results. The three examples are not a complete semantic
audit. A factual evaluation method needs explicit design and review before it
can replace the shared quality policy.
